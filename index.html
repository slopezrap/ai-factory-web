AI Factory

A personal tool to prepare and publish short product videos.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it is

AI Factory is a private dashboard used by its owner to prepare short videos about affiliate products and to publish them to the owner's own social accounts. It runs on the owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no public sign-up.

How it uses TikTok

With Login Kit, the owner connects their own TikTok account. With Content Posting API, the owner uploads a finished video to their own TikTok inbox as a draft, to review and post from the TikTok app.

How it uses Instagram

The owner connects their own professional Instagram account to publish their own videos and read the statistics of their own posts.
