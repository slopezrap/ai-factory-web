Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Who we are

AI Factory is a private tool operated by a single individual (the "owner"). It is used only by the owner, on the owner's own computer. Contact: s.lopezrap@gmail.com.

What data we access

TikTok. When the owner connects TikTok through Login Kit, the app receives an access token and the basic profile information of that same account (open ID, display name, avatar), under the user.info.basic scope. It uses the video.upload scope to send the owner's videos to that account as drafts.

Instagram. When the owner connects their professional Instagram account, the app receives an access token and the basic information of that account, publishes the owner's own videos, and reads the statistics of the owner's own posts.

The app does not read other users' data, does not collect data from viewers or followers, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted, in a database on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any third party other than the platform they belong to. The videos are the owner's own content.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose personal data to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the dashboard deletes its stored token at once. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform: in TikTok, Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services; in Instagram, Settings > Apps and websites. To ask for any data to be deleted, write to s.lopezrap@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
